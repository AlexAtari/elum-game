# Version 1 Scope

Version 1 includes:

- 2–4 players
- Computer opponents
- Procedurally generated hex maps
- Fog of war
- Land acquisition
- Universal harvesters
- Food, energy, ore and crystals
- Population growth
- Dynamic market
- Global and local events
- 20–30 minute matches

Version 1 does not include:

- Corporations
- Stock market
- Research tree
- Diplomacy
- Campaign
- Online multiplayer
- Land resale
- Complex buildings
